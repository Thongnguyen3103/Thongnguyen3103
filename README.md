### Professional Work

#### AnPhuoc Group — Business Data & Analytics
**Sales and inventory analytics for retail operations**

Working repository for analysis supporting the sales department: demand planning, inventory balancing, and performance reporting across stores.

- Build weekly and monthly replenishment forecasts per store, based on sell-through rate, current stock position, and seasonality.
- Segment products by sales performance (best-seller / slow-moving) across category, style, size, and location to guide purchasing.
- Analyze revenue and gross margin by price tier (full price / promotion / clearance) and evaluate campaign effectiveness.
- Plan inter-store and warehouse-to-store transfers to raise sell-through, reduce aged inventory, and prevent size and style stockouts.
- Recommend markdown levels for aged stock, balancing cash recovery against margin.
- Replaced a fully manual reporting process with coded automation, **cutting report preparation time by roughly 70%**.
- Maintain automated Power BI and Excel reporting delivered to management and store teams.

> Note: this repository contains analysis methods and tooling only. No confidential commercial data is published.
